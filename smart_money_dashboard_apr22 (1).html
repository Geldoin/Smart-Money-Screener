Smart Money Flow Scanner — How it works
This dashboard identifies sector rotation and Smart Money inflows in real time. It scans a curated universe of high-relative-strength stocks — filtered daily via TradingView — and groups them by industry to reveal where institutional capital is concentrating.

By aggregating above-avg relative volume and above-avg 1W% at the industry level, the scanner surfaces coordinated buying — a classic footprint of Smart Money rotation. Database: 1,007 tickers · 10 sessions (Apr 9–22) · 100% complete, no truncation.
